## Licensing note (Unsplash+)

The three `premium_photo-…` assets are Unsplash+ (paid-tier) images.
Their license differs from the free Unsplash license. The project
owner must confirm the project holds the right to self-host these
three photos before deploying them; if not, replace them with
properly licensed alternatives. They are flagged individually in the
Phase 2 manifest (`unsplash-manifest.json` in the reports folder).
